## 1. Content rating (IARC questionnaire)

- **Email for IARC certificates:** `«[يُستكمل: البريد الإلكتروني للتواصل]»`
- **Category:** **Reference, News, or Educational**. The app is an educational and reference library of Islamic lectures. It is not a game, social or communication app, or entertainment app.

Answers. Every question is **No**:

| Question (abridged) | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does the app contain violence (realistic, fantasy, blood, gore)? | **No** (see the caveat in `apple-age-rating.md`: spoken, non-graphic historical narration) |
| Fear or horror content? | **No** |
| Sexuality, nudity, or sexual references? | **No** |
| Crude humor or profanity / bad language? | **No** |
| References to or depictions of illegal drugs, alcohol, or tobacco? | **No** |
| Promotes or depicts gambling, or simulated gambling? | **No** |
| Discriminatory or hate content? | **No** |
| Does the app let users interact or exchange content (chat, UGC, sharing location with other users)? | **No**. Notes are private and local, and community is dev-only |
| Does the app share the user's current location with other users? | **No** |
| Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods? | **No** |
| Does the app contain unrestricted internet access (a browser)? | **No**. It opens fixed external links only |
| Is the app primarily a news app? | **No** |

**Expected ratings:** ESRB **Everyone** · PEGI **3** · USK **0** · ClassInd **L** · ACB **G** · GRAC **All** · IARC generic **3+**.

## 3. App access

**All functionality is available without special access.** There is no login, account, membership, or region or IP gate. A reviewer needs no credentials. Notes for the reviewer, to paste:

> No account is required. Lecture audio streams from archive.org and needs an internet connection. Location permission is optional: it is used only to show prayer times (More → Settings → مواقيت الصلاة).

## 4. Ads

**No, my app does not contain ads.** There is no ad SDK, no house ads, and no sponsored content.
